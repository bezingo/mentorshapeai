# Product Mission

## Pitch

MentorShape is an AI-powered mentoring platform that automates the entire mentoring lifecycle—goal creation, mentor matching, session planning, collaboration, progress tracking, and actionable insights. The platform helps individuals achieve their goals through structured guidance, collaborative sessions, and expert mentorship while enabling organizations to run scalable mentorship programs.

The system works in a **Linktree-style format** where every user (mentor or mentee) has a unique public link showcasing their profile, goals, or expertise.

## Vision

The platform prioritizes:
- **Ease of onboarding** via LinkedIn or CV parsing
- **Goal clarity and milestone planning** through AI
- **Frictionless collaboration** between mentors and mentees
- **Structured sessions** powered by AI agendas and summaries
- **Ecosystem for mentors** to showcase expertise and monetize their time
- **Organizational control** for schools, universities, and companies to manage internal mentoring programs

## Users

### Primary Customers

- **Individual Mentees**: Professionals, students, and career changers seeking structured guidance to achieve specific goals (career transitions, skill development, startup building, personal growth)
- **Individual Mentors**: Experienced professionals looking to share expertise, build a personal brand, and monetize their knowledge through consultations and digital products
- **Organizations**: Universities, companies, and institutions running structured mentorship programs for students, employees, or community members

## The Problem

### Unstructured Mentorship Leads to Abandoned Goals

Traditional mentorship lacks structure, accountability, and follow-through. Mentees struggle to articulate goals, mentors lack tools to track progress, and sessions often lack direction. 70% of informal mentoring relationships fail within the first few months due to unclear expectations and poor communication.

**Our Solution:** AI-powered goal shaping, milestone planning, session agendas, and progress tracking that transforms casual mentoring into a structured, outcome-driven experience with clear accountability for both parties.

### Organizations Cannot Scale Mentorship Programs

Universities and companies want to run mentorship programs but face operational challenges: manual matching based on spreadsheets, no visibility into relationship health, and difficulty measuring outcomes. Program administrators spend 40% of their time on administrative tasks instead of program improvement.

**Our Solution:** AI-driven mentor-mentee matching, automated collaboration lifecycle management, and comprehensive dashboards that give organizations complete visibility and control over their programs.

### Mentors Have No Platform to Monetize Expertise

Experienced professionals want to mentor but lack a unified platform to showcase expertise, manage availability, deliver structured sessions, and get compensated for their time. Existing solutions are fragmented across Calendly, Zoom, Stripe, and Notion.

**Our Solution:** Linktree-style mentor pages with integrated scheduling, session management, paid consultations, and digital product sales—all in one platform with built-in AI assistance.

## Differentiators

### AI-First Mentorship Lifecycle

Unlike generic meeting schedulers or learning platforms, MentorShape embeds AI into every step of the mentoring journey:
- **Profile Builder Agent:** Scrapes LinkedIn/CV and structures user data
- **Goal Shaper Agent:** Expands goals into milestones, success criteria, and weekly breakdowns
- **Session Planner Agent:** Generates pre-session agendas with questions and prep suggestions
- **Transcript Summarizer Agent:** Extracts action items, key decisions, and milestone updates
- **Progress Tracker Agent:** Scores progress, identifies risks, and sends nudges
- **Matching Agent:** Creates ranked mentor-mentee pairs with explainable match scores
- **Completion Agent:** Generates final summaries and shareable LinkedIn posts

This results in 3x faster goal clarity, 50% reduction in session prep time, and measurable progress tracking.

### Linktree-Style Public Profiles

Unlike closed mentorship platforms, every mentor and goal has a shareable public page:
- **Mentor pages** at `/m/[handle]` — showcase expertise, testimonials, paid offerings
- **Goal pages** at `/g/[slug]` — display mentee goals to attract mentor collaborations

This enables organic growth where each user becomes a distribution channel.

### Unified Platform for All Stakeholders

Unlike point solutions that serve only mentees OR mentors OR organizations, MentorShape provides a complete ecosystem:
- Individuals can seamlessly toggle between mentee and mentor roles
- Organizations manage institutional programs with the same underlying infrastructure
- Mentors can participate in both individual and org-sponsored programs

## Core Modules

### 1. Mentee Suite
- Create goals (30 or 60-day cycles)
- Share public goal page with mentors
- Book sessions based on mentor availability
- Receive AI-generated agendas and action items
- Track progress via milestones and check-ins
- Complete goals and get AI-reviewed achievement summaries

### 2. Mentor Suite
- Create a public mentor page (Linktree-style portfolio)
- Showcase areas of expertise, skills & mentorship topics
- Accept collab requests from goal pages
- Configure weekly availability connected to Google/Microsoft Calendar
- Deliver sessions and access AI agendas/summaries
- Offer paid consultations with Stripe Connect
- Sell digital products (Notion templates, guides, PDFs)
- Earn badges for completed mentorships

### 3. Organization Suite
- Manage org-level mentorship programs
- Invite mentors (faculty, alumni, managers) and mentees (students, employees)
- Use AI matching to connect mentors ↔ mentees
- Track progress through dashboards
- View program health and completion metrics
- Facilitate collab lifecycle within the org

### 4. AI Layer
AI agents power multiple steps:
- Profile scraping & parsing
- Goal shaping and milestone creation
- Session agenda planning
- Transcript summarization and action-item extraction
- AI-based mentor matching with embeddings
- Progress scoring and risk prediction

## Revenue Model

### Mentee Revenue
- **Free Plan:** 1 active goal, 1 active collaboration, limited AI features
- **Pro Plan:** Multiple goals, unlimited collaborations, unlimited AI features, priority support

### Organization Revenue
- **Starter:** 100 mentees
- **Pro:** 500 mentees
- **Enterprise:** Unlimited mentees + SSO + custom domain + dedicated success manager

### Mentor Revenue (Platform Fees)
Mentors use the platform free. Platform earns through:
- **Paid Consultations:** 10-15% platform fee on mentor-defined consultation packages
- **Digital Products:** 10-15% platform fee on guides, templates, and courses sold by mentors
