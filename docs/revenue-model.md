# Revenue Model

## 1. Mentee Revenue

### Free Plan
- 1 active goal
- 1 active collaboration
- Limited AI agendas
- Basic summaries

### Pro Plan (Subscription)
- Multiple goals
- Unlimited collaborations
- Unlimited sessions
- Unlimited agendas & summaries
- AI progress tracking
- Priority support

---

## 2. Organization Revenue
Plans:
- Starter (100 mentees)
- Pro (500 mentees)
- Enterprise (unlimited)
- Add-ons:
  - Custom domain
  - SSO
  - Enhanced reporting
  - Dedicated success manager

Billing:
- Stripe subscription at org level

---

## 3. Mentor Revenue
Mentors use platform free.

Platform earns through:
### A. Paid Consultations
Mentor defines:
- Price
- Duration
- Niche

Platform fee:
- 10–15%

### B. Digital Products
Mentors sell:
- Guides
- Templates
- Playbooks
- PDFs
- Courses

Platform fee:
- 10–15%

### C. Organization Programs
Mentors may earn from company-sponsored programs (optional future model)
